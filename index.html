ClearAnalytics studio

A private tool for the YouTube channels of its owner: it reads their analytics today and will publish the owner's own videos to them later.

What it does

ClearAnalytics studio runs on its owner's computer. After the owner signs in with Google, it reads, without changing anything:

  • the basic details of the owner's channel (channel ID, title and handle);
  • the list of videos uploaded to that channel, with their titles, privacy status and scheduled publish time;
  • YouTube Analytics reports for those videos: views, engaged views, watch time, average view duration, likes, comments, shares, subscribers gained and audience retention.

It uses this data to match each uploaded video with the owner's own production records and to show the owner how each video performs 1, 3, 7, 14 and 28 days after it is published, with a weekly summary.

Publishing (planned). Later, ClearAnalytics studio will upload the owner's finished videos to the owner's own channels, with their title, description and scheduled publish time, so the owner does not have to upload each one by hand.

Why it asks for access

Today ClearAnalytics studio requests two read-only permissions: View your YouTube account (to find the channel's uploaded videos) and View YouTube Analytics reports for your YouTube content (to read their performance). With them it cannot upload, edit or delete anything.

When publishing is turned on, it will also ask for permission to upload videos. It will use that permission only to upload the owner's own videos and to set their title, description and publish time. This page and the privacy policy will describe any new permission before the app asks for it.

Who can use it

ClearAnalytics studio is not offered to the public. It is used only by its owner, for channels the owner manages.
