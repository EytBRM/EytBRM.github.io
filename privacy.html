Privacy Policy

ClearAnalytics studio · last updated 23 November 2026

ClearAnalytics studio is a private tool that its owner runs on their own computer to read the analytics of their own YouTube channels and, later, to publish the owner's own videos to them. It is not offered to the public. This policy explains what data it accesses and what it does with it.

YouTube API Services

ClearAnalytics studio uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data ClearAnalytics studio accesses

Only after you sign in with Google and grant read-only access, ClearAnalytics studio reads:

  • the basic details of your channel: channel ID, title and handle;
  • the videos uploaded to your channel: video ID, title, privacy status, upload time and scheduled publish time;
  • YouTube Analytics reports for those videos: views, engaged views, estimated minutes watched, average view duration, likes, comments, shares, subscribers gained and audience retention.

It does not read your email address, contacts, comments' text, private messages or any data of other people. With these read-only permissions it cannot upload, edit or delete anything on YouTube.

How the data is used

  • To match each uploaded video with the owner's own production records.
  • To record how each video performs 1, 3, 7, 14 and 28 days after it is published, so the owner can compare videos.
  • To prepare a weekly summary for the owner. The figures in that summary (numbers such as views and retention, without Google account credentials) may be processed by an AI model provider, Anthropic, to draft the summary text for the owner.

Publishing (planned)

When publishing is turned on, ClearAnalytics studio will also ask for permission to upload videos. It will use that permission only to upload the owner's own finished videos to the owner's own channels and to set their title, description and scheduled publish time. The videos and their text come from the owner's computer; the app does not read any other data with this permission and does not delete videos. This policy will be updated before the app asks for that permission.

Storage and security

The data and the sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, in files readable only by the owner's user account. ClearAnalytics studio has no server, uses no cookies and no tracking, and shows no ads.

Sharing

ClearAnalytics studio does not sell data, does not use it for advertising and does not share it with anyone, except the processing of summary figures described above and where the law requires it.

ClearAnalytics studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention, revoking access and deletion

The data is kept for as long as the owner uses ClearAnalytics studio to compare videos. You can revoke ClearAnalytics studio' access to your Google account at any time on the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. After access is revoked, the stored tokens and the data read with them are deleted within 7 days. You can also ask for deletion at any time using the contact below.
